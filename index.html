Calcs

A calculator that grows with you, plus the everyday tools you reach for next. Free, offline and private.

For Android 10 and newer

Everything in one app

Calculator

Arithmetic keys, with scientific keys one tap away. Exact decimal maths, a live result, brackets closed for you, DEG and RAD, and a history of your last 100 calculations.

Unit converter

12 categories from length and temperature to pressure, energy, power and number systems. Search like "kg to lb", save favorites, see the formula.

Currency converter

154 currencies with rates built in, so it works offline. Update the rates when you want, convert one amount into many currencies, and see a conversion table.

Percentage, date, BMI, loan

Percent of, change and add or take off. Days between dates. BMI with a clear scale. Loan and EMI payments with a yearly schedule.

Looks the way you like

Five accent colours in light, dark and pure-black AMOLED, or follow your phone. Works in portrait and landscape, on phones and tablets.

Home-screen widgets

Resizable arithmetic and scientific calculator widgets that use the same engine as the app.

A look inside

Private by design

  • No ads, no account, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your history, favorites and settings stay on your phone.
  • The only time the app uses the internet is to download exchange rates, when you ask it to (or automatically on Wi-Fi if you allow it).
